\title[Do Public BDD Suites Still Work as Oracles?]{Do Public BDD Suites Still Work as Oracles?
A Mutation-Based Instrument for Detecting Executable-Specification Drift in
Cucumber-Style Suites}

% Anonymised for double-anonymous review (iSys policy).
\author[Anonymous submission]{\affil{\textbf{Anonymous submission --- author identities withheld for review}}}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract-en}
[Context] A business rule lives in three places that can drift apart: the organisational requirement,
the executable specification written in Gherkin, and the code. Behaviour-Driven Development (BDD) is
adopted on the premise that executable specifications keep the first and third aligned, yet no metric
is used to check whether that alignment still holds. [Objective] We ask whether the
specification layer of a Cucumber-style suite still functions as an \emph{oracle} --- whether it can
discriminate a change in the business rules it claims to describe --- and we design an instrument for
organisations to answer it about their own suites. [Method] Following Design Science
Research, we build and evaluate a drift-detection protocol as feasibility of mutation-based
oracle measurement in two ecosystems, and a measurement of the public population from a frame of \Nframe{}
repositories drawn with a fixed seed under a categorised attrition protocol.
[Results] Mutation-based oracle measurement is feasible in the Java and JavaScript/TypeScript
ecosystems, with per-mutant coverage and per-scenario attribution. However, of
\Nscreened{} screened repositories \Neligible{} met the study's static eligibility criteria
(\YieldPct\,\%; 95\,\% CI \YieldLo--\YieldHi\,\%), and of \Nbuildtested{} build-tested repositories
\Ngreen{} produced a green, executing Gherkin baseline. [Conclusion/Contributions] The binding constraint on evidence about BDD oracle quality is
infrastructural rather than statistical: public BDD suites are overwhelmingly frameworks, templates and
demonstrators, not applications with an executable specification layer. We contribute the instrument, the feasibility evidence and a
quantified attrition analysis. \emph{Limitation.} No independent human raters were available, so the
judgement-coded indicators carry no inter-rater reliability statistic, and no corpus-scale association
could be estimated.
\end{abstract-en}
